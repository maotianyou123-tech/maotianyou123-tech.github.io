Robust Attitude Controller Design for Bi-copter on SO(3)

Tianyou Mao (First author), Bosong Duan, Chuangqiang Guo

International Journal of Control, Automation and Systems, 2025
Publisher PDF

Overview

This paper addresses coupled, fast-changing attitude tracking for an underactuated bi-copter with non-minimum-phase dynamics. It combines an extended state observer with a backstepping controller formulated directly on SO(3).

Main contributions

  1. SO(3) observer and backstepping control

    An extended state observer estimates lumped uncertainty, while a backstepping controller tracks the attitude matrix without relying on a small-angle Euler approximation.

  2. Closed-loop stability analysis

    Lyapunov-based analysis establishes bounded stability of the observer, controller, and combined system under the stated disturbance assumptions.

  3. Tracking beyond the hover neighborhood

    Comparative simulations and prototype experiments examine rapid, cross-axis attitude commands and disturbance rejection, extending the demonstrated attitude range beyond near-hover operation.
