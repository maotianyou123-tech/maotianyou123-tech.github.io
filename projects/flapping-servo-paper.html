Motion Attitude and Aerodynamic Characteristics Research of Flapping Wings Driven by Micro Servoactuator

Tianyou Mao (First author), Bosong Duan, Bihui Yin, Chuangqiang Guo

Journal of Bionic Engineering, 2024
Publisher PDF

Overview

This study examines how micro-servo motion commands affect flapping-wing thrust and energy use. It links actuator response, flexible-wing dynamics, and electrical power to guide the choice of flapping waveform, frequency, and preset amplitude.

Main contributions

  1. Modeling amplitude loss in servo-driven flapping

    An Amplitude Loss Model relates the commanded waveform and motion parameters to the actual wing stroke, accounting for finite servo response during repeated reversals.

  2. Coupled thrust, power, and power-loading prediction

    The Flapping Wing Dynamic Model and Power Load Model include amplitude attenuation and advanced wing twisting to connect motion commands with aerodynamic and energetic performance.

  3. Motion-parameter optimization

    The experiments support sinusoidal command waveforms combined with adaptive preset amplitude, providing a model-based way to improve thrust while considering power loading.

  4. Validation using three measured quantities

    Motion capture, force sensing, and power measurements validate the models. Within the tested common frequency range, reported absolute percentage errors are below 10% for thrust, 5% for power, and 13% for power loading.
