Effect of flapping mode on aerodynamic characteristics of bat-like flapping-wing aerial vehicle

Bosong Duan, Tianyou Mao, Kening Gong, Chuangqiang Guo, Hong Liu

2022 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Biomimetics (ROBIO), 2022
Publisher PDF

Overview

This paper compares symmetric and two asymmetric flapping modes for a bat-like aerial vehicle. It combines linkage optimization with coupled fluid–structure simulations to study how the stroke pattern affects transmission loading, lift, thrust, and pitching moment.

Main contributions

  1. Mode-specific mechanism optimization

    A double-crank, double-rocker mechanism synchronizes the two wings. A hybrid genetic–particle swarm algorithm selects linkage dimensions for each stroke pattern while minimizing peak angular acceleration; the symmetric mode produces the lowest peak acceleration in the reported comparison.

  2. Coupled simulation of flexible wings

    Abaqus and XFlow exchange aerodynamic loads and wing deformation to compare the three modes using the same flexible wing. The study evaluates a reference condition of 10 Hz, 15° angle of attack, and 5 m/s inflow, then varies the angle of attack and inflow speed.

  3. Quantified aerodynamic trade-offs

    At the reference condition, UR reduces mean pitching moment by about 36% relative to symmetric flapping, while U increases mean thrust by about 12.6%. Across the tested cases, UR favors lower pitching moment, U favors thrust, and symmetric flapping provides an intermediate compromise, offering guidance for selecting a stroke pattern.

Summary based on the published paper, pp. 1086–1091. Reported comparisons are from mechanism calculations and fluid–structure simulations.
